Optimizing sampling and monitoring of species interactions within Biodiversity Observation Networks

Gabriel Dansereau, Michael D. Catchen, Ceres Barros, Timothée Poisot
Journal of Animal Ecology · 2026-10-23 · doi:10.1111/1365-2656.70378
TerrestrialMarineInteraction classificationMonitoringMethods / toolsimulationnetwork optimisation#biodiversity observation networks#sampling design#interaction networks

Simulations of Biodiversity Observation Networks show the sampling effort needed is considerably higher for interactions than for species, especially when realising and detecting an interaction depends on abundance. The good news: for a focal species, placing sites using its known range recovered its interactions efficiently.

🧂 Caveats: Simulation only. Automated pipelines add classifier error on top of the sampling problem.
