The Target Is a Predicate: Predicate-State Grounding for Semantic-Level Video Reasoning Segmentation

Yuhuan Hong, Yiming Ren, Zhiqiang Lin, Feize Wu, Xingyun Liu, Yitong Xie, Lei Zhou, Lisheng Wu, Junjie Wang, Yujiu Yang
arXiv · 2026-11-10
Video / imageryTextInteraction classificationMethods / toolPreprintvideo segmentationSAM2vision-language models#temporal grounding#target switching#benchmark

Hong … Yang define queries as predicates over objects and time (think “the fish currently being cleaned”), so the target can be absent or switch identity. Their PS-Bench has 2,077 videos and 16,250 queries with active/vacant intervals and masks, and their State-Bind-Emit pipeline prompts SAM2 only during active states: +9.32 J&F over baselines and far fewer false masks.
