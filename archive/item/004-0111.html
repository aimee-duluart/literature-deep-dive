An autonomous imaging system for reef fish monitoring in the southern Gulf of Mexico

Edlin José Guerra-Castro, Miguel Ángel Plata-Díaz, Víctor Eduardo Gómez-Bretón, Damián Alejandro Cedillo-Reyna, Rodrigo Contreras-Chávez, Arturo Sanchez-Porras, Ilse Ruiz-Mercado, Vanesa Papiol, Ximena Isabel Pérez-Meza, Yoalli Quetzalli Hernández-Díaz, Alan Javier Ramírez-Menéndez, Ángela Randazzo-Eisemann, Nuno Simoes

Coral reefMarineVideo / imagerySpecies detectionMonitoringMethods / toolPeer reviewedFishRF-DETRobject detectiontrail cameras#autonomous imaging#Gulf of Mexico#reef fish assemblages

Guerra-Castro … Simões deployed five adapted trail cameras on Bajo de Diez reef (southern Gulf of Mexico) in 2024 and 2025: 191,758 usable images at 3-min intervals, with 27,471 expert-annotated. RF-DETR reached mAP@50 = 0.741, and its temporal community structure correlated with the experts' (ρ = 0.63), stretching coverage from 68 to 110 dates.
