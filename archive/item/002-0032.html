Recruitment pulses cause a market dilemma in cleaning interactions from a tropical Atlantic rocky reef

Gabriel S Garcia, Ana C Suhett, Aléxia A Lessa, Juliana G Mello-Fonseca, Juan P Quimbayo, Murilo S Dias
Behavioral Ecology · 2026-10-31 · doi:10.1093/beheco/arag131
MarineInteraction classificationEmpirical / fieldFishnetwork analysisunderwater surveys#cleaning mutualism#biological markets#recruitment#rocky reef

Garcia, Suhett, Lessa, Mello-Fonseca, Quimbayo & Dias followed cleaning interactions on a rocky reef at Arraial do Cabo (SW Atlantic) for three years. The dedicated cleaner goby Elacatinus figaro was the most important cleaner in the network despite low abundance.
