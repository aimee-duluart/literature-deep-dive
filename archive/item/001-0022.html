Hydromorphological Restoration of Streams Does Not Ensure the Recovery of Ecosystem Functioning

Ulrike Haase, Daniel C. Allen, Christine Anlanger, Iola G. Boëchat, Clara Mendoza‐Lera, Wyatt F. Cross, Pia Ebeling, Davi G. Fernandes Cunha, Patrick Fink, Nicolas Finkler, Björn Gücker, Andreas Lorke, Julia Pasqualini, Christopher J. Patrick, Marc Peipoch, Oskar Schröder, Ryan Sponseller, Daniel von Schiller, Markus Weitere, Mario Brauns
Global Change Biology · 2026-10 · doi:10.1111/gcb.71126
FreshwaterReviewmeta-analysis#restoration#ecosystem functioning#null result

Haase et al. pooled 582 effect sizes from 66 studies. Gross primary production was higher in degraded than reference streams, denitrification was lower in restored than degraded ones, and restoration didn't consistently reverse functional differences.
