Long-term impacts of deep-sea mining on sediment organic matter quantity and quality

Rachel M. Jeffreys, Louisa Norman, Sabena J. Blackbird, Bethany F. M. Fleming, Bryan O’Malley, Adrian G. Glover, Daniel O. B. Jones
Elem Sci Anth · 2026-11-02 · doi:10.1525/elementa.2025.00106
Deep seaMarineVideo / imageryMonitoringEmpirical / fieldPeer reviewedsediment samplinglipid biomarkersstable isotopes#deep-sea mining#Clarion-Clipperton Zone#recovery

Jeffreys and colleagues (SMARTEX) sampled a 1979 nodule-mining test site in the Clarion-Clipperton Zone at about 4,800 m. They found lower quantity and quality of sediment organic matter than at an undisturbed control site, linked to the lower animal abundances seen in SMARTEX imagery. doi:10.1525/elementa.2025.00106 · ON&T news
