HMMF-Net: A Lightweight Hierarchical Multi-Frame Multimodal Fusion Network for Accurate Fish Feeding Intensity Assessment

Phi-Hung Hoang, Van-Manh Tran, Nam-Thuan Trinh, Thi-Thu-Hong Phan
arXiv · 2026-11-06
AquacultureVideo / imageryAcousticsMultimodalBehaviour classificationMethods / toolPreprintFishaudio-visual fusionEfficientNetedge deployment#feeding behaviour#cross-modal fusion#aquaculture

Hoang, Tran, Trinh & Phan fuse stacked video frames with log-mel spectrograms through compact “messenger tokens” rather than heavy cross-attention. On the AV-FFIA dataset: 96.77% accuracy with 3.99M parameters, cutting classification error by 28% against the best video-only setup, and 11.5 ms per 2-s clip on a Jetson Orin Nano.
