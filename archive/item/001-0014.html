Field testing the influence of settlement recesses and nighttime feeding lights on coral recruitment

Hendrikje Jorissen, Jessica Reichert, Shannon L. Dixon, Esme M. Madin, Charlotte A. Madin, Mollie Asbury, Allison D. Nims, Marina E. Rottmueller, Lomani H. Rova, The R3D Consortium, Andréa G. Grottoli, Joshua S. Madin
Scientific Reports · 2026-10-22 · doi:10.1038/s41598-026-63624-7
Coral reefMarineMonitoringEmpirical / fieldCoralsfield experiment#coral recruitment#restoration modules#Hawaii

Jorissen, Reichert, Dixon & Madin ran a 6-month test on concrete restoration modules in Kāneʻohe Bay. Recesses dramatically improved initial larval entrainment, and survival at 6 months was higher in deeper recesses.
