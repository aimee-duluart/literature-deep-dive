eDNA metabarcoding and random forest identify super-connector fish species across mangrove–seagrass–coral reef continuums in the South China Sea

Chunchun Liu, Gongguo Wu, Chujun Zou, Donghai Wu, Pengfei Xie, Sai Wang, Ping Wang
Frontiers in Ecology and Evolution · 2026-11-09 · doi:10.3389/fevo.2026.1960019
Coral reefMarineeDNAMonitoringEmpirical / fieldPeer reviewedFisheDNA metabarcodingrandom forestbipartite networks#seascape connectivity#connector species#South China Sea

Liu … Wang sampled a continuum on Hainan Island and detected 234 fish species, about four times the 58 found by gillnets. Of 23 connector species, three (Lutjanus russellii, Siganus fuscescens, Gerres oyena) carried 47% of cross-habitat network flow, and simulated removal cut connectivity by 68%.
