A decade of multi-site acoustic monitoring reveals acoustic co-occurrence of finless porpoises and high-frequency vessel noise

Mayu I. Ogawa, Satoko S. Kimura, Nozomu Ishiai, Hirotaka Tajima, Haruka Nakajin, Ken Yoda, Nobuaki Arai, Tomonari Akamatsu
Ecological Indicators · 2026-11-14 · doi:10.1016/j.ecolind.2026.115847
MarineAcousticsMonitoringEmpirical / fieldPeer reviewedMarine mammalspassive acoustic monitoringhurdle models#vessel noise#finless porpoise#Mikawa Bay

Ogawa … Akamatsu analysed 2013–2023 PAM data from ten stations in Mikawa Bay, Japan. Porpoises were heard on 99.3% of days and high-frequency vessel noise on 94.8%.
