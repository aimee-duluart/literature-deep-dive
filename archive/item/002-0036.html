Overexploitation risks disproportionate vertebrate downsizing and declines in potential dispersal distances in frugivory networks

Oscar Morton, Miguel Lurgi, David P. Edwards
Proceedings of the Royal Society B: Biological Sciences · 2026-10-28 · doi:10.1098/rspb.2026.0578
TerrestrialInteraction classificationEmpirical / fieldPeer reviewedBirdsMammalsextinction simulationnetwork analysis#frugivory networks#overexploitation#seed dispersal#defaunation

Morton, Lurgi & Edwards simulated losing overexploited species from 160 frugivory networks (>16,000 interactions). Body-mass downsizing was six-fold greater than under random loss.
