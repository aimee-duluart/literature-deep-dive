How Finely Can Vision-Language Models See Biology? Bio-VQA: A Benchmark for Taxonomic, Morphological, Spatial and Individual Reasoning

S M Rayeed, Qian Ma, Charles V. Stewart, Yao Ma
arXiv · 2026-11-03
TerrestrialMarineVideo / imageryMultimodalSpecies detectionMonitoringDatasetPreprintvision-language modelsvisual question answeringbenchmarking#counting#re-identification#fine-grained taxonomy#BioCLIP

A benchmark of 1.86M questions over 217K images from 11 public datasets, covering taxonomy, traits, counting and individual re-identification. It uses graded scoring, so a near-miss scores better than a wild guess.

🧂 Caveats: Preprint. Open-weight models only, so no GPT-class or Gemini-class systems. Adversarial re-ID is a deliberately hard setting.
