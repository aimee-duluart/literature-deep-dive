A common marking method influences fish-parasite interactions

Jessica Stephenson
openRxiv · 2026-10-29 · doi:10.64898/2026.10.27.762165
FreshwaterInteraction classificationEmpirical / labFishVIE markingexperimental infection#host-parasite interactions#marking effects#methodological bias

Visible Implant Elastomer (VIE) tags are a standard way to tell fish apart, and earlier studies found no effect on survival, growth or behaviour. This study compared 26 VIE-marked with 46 control guppies.

🧂 Caveats: Single-author preprint, one host-parasite system, modest sample sizes.
