MICE: Multi-animal Interaction Context Encoder - A Hierarchical Foundation Model for Mouse Behavior

Yen-Shuo Su, Weihan Li, Anqi Wu
arXiv · 2026-11-08
Video / imageryInteraction classificationBehaviour classificationMethods / toolPreprintMammalsself-supervised learningfoundation modelsPerceiverpose estimation#social behaviour#multi-animal interactions#variable group size

Most self-supervised pose models encode each animal separately, so the interaction signal (who approached, chased or groomed whom) gets thrown away. They also hard-code how many animals are in view.

🧂 Caveats: Lab mice in arenas, with pose already extracted. The authors say the architecture makes no mouse-specific assumptions, but that's untested on other species. Interactions here are within one species (aggression, courtship, allogrooming), not predator–prey or cleaning. A preprint.
