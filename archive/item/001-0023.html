Dispersal corridors enhance modelled near-bed larval connectivity across deep-sea protected area networks

Molly Kendall James, Amelia Elizabeth Henrietta Bridges, Daniel O. B. Jones, Charlotte Hannah Clubley, Antony Mark Knights, Danielle S. W. DeJonge, Kerry Louise Howell
Nature Ecology &amp; Evolution · 2026-10-23 · doi:10.1038/s41559-026-03172-5
MarineEmpirical / fieldLagrangian dispersal modellingnetwork analysis#deep-sea mining#larval connectivity#Clarion-Clipperton Zone#protected areas

James, Bridges, Jones, Clubley et al. found fewer than 0.02% of modelled larvae reach another protected area (APEI) under realistic near-bed dispersal of 19–69 days. Strategic corridors increased inter-APEI dispersal 406-fold.
