A million orthopteran singers from a year of continuously operating field recorders detected, tracked and organized by song

Michael B. Eisen
openRxiv · 2026-10-21 · doi:10.64898/2026.10.18.759993
TerrestrialAcousticsSpecies detectionMonitoringMethods / toolInvertebratesunsupervised detectionacoustic trackingARUs#insect song#orthoptera#soundscape#temperature

Insect choruses lack discrete calls and labelled libraries, so this method exploits repetitive song instead. It separates singers in minute windows, links them across a night as frequency and syllable rate drift with temperature, and builds a catalogue across days and sites.

🧂 Caveats: Single-author preprint from one site. It relies on repetitive song, so it won't transfer directly to irregular reef-fish sounds. Song types aren't species yet.
