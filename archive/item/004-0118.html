Shuck it and see: Adaptive Restoration Engineering in community‐led shellfish reef restoration

Robert J. Porter, Marina A. Richardson, Craig Copeland, Rebecca C. Porter, Sophie DiBetta, Gary Gilchrist, Graham Toms, David Hastings, Ben L. Gilby
Restoration Ecology · 2026-11-11 · doi:10.1111/rec.70597
MarineReviewPeer reviewedInvertebratesfield observationdesign iteration#shellfish reef restoration#adaptive management#community-led restoration

Porter … Gilchrist assessed 1,081 remnant reef bommies at five Queensland sites and traced how five structure designs (2018–2026) changed as things failed: corroding mesh, burial, subsidence, and mussels recruiting where oysters were expected. The result is “Adaptive Restoration Engineering”: no standard unit, just designs refined for each setting.
