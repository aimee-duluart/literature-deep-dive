From pose to behavior: SABER integrates identity-resolved multi-animal pose tracking with large-language-model-based behavioral factor discovery

Jiaqi Zheng, Chen Peng, Shi-Yuan Zhang, Jie Wang, Wen-Ning Zhou, Hong-Mei Xue, Tian-Qi Cai, Yi-Wen Li, Xiao-Ya Su, Zhiwei Jiang, Yangyi Tan, Xiang-Dong Sun

Zheng, Peng, Zhang et al. couple multi-animal pose estimation with identity-preserving tracking, then mine interpretable kinematic, postural and social descriptors and classify behaviour over multiple timescales from one overhead camera. It beat comparator pipelines on pose accuracy and tracking continuity across social, mating and aggression recordings in mice.
