Deep learning reveals persistent individual signatures in bat echolocation calls of the greater leaf-nosed bat

Li AQ, Huang WT, Xie XY, Wen WM, Ji L, Zhang HJ, Zhang CY, Luo JH
Zoological Research · 2026-11-01 · doi:10.24272/j.issn.2095-8137.2025.642
TerrestrialAcousticsSpecies detectionEmpirical / labPeer reviewedMammalsCNNdiscriminant function analysis#individual identification#echolocation#bats

Li, Huang, Xie et al. recorded 17,584 calls from 34 greater leaf-nosed bats. A CNN identified individuals at 84% (single calls) and 91% (sequences), against 39% and 47% for discriminant function analysis.
