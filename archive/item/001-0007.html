Sound advice: A calibration framework for defining detection space in passive acoustic monitoring

Prabhat Sharma, Kunapareddy Kezia, Kadaba Shamanna Seshadri
PLOS One · 2026-10-27 · doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0350789
TerrestrialAcousticsMonitoringEmpirical / fieldplayback experimentARU arraysdetection modelling#detection range#study design#PAM calibration

Pure-tone playbacks (1–8 kHz) over 50–300 m in the Western Ghats found 1 kHz signals dropped to 50% detection at about 100 m, while 4–6 kHz stayed reliable to 250 m. Recorder orientation mattered hugely: 81% detection facing the source versus 14% rear-facing.
