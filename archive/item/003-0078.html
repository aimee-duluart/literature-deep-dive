How many labels do you need? A decision framework for cross‐habitat marine biodiversity monitoring

Alzayat Saleh, Mostafa Rahimi Azghadi
Ecological Solutions and Evidence · 2026-11-06 · doi:10.1002/2688-8319.70348
Coral reefMarineVideo / imagerySpecies detectionMethods / toolPeer reviewedFishCoralsInvertebratesDINOv2CLIPlinear probingLoRAfew-shot#labelling budget#cross-site transfer#domain shift

Saleh & Rahimi Azghadi ran 900+ transfer experiments across reefs (GBR, French Polynesia) and a Danish fjord. Frozen DINOv2 + a linear classifier (1,538 trainable parameters) generalised to unseen reef sites at least as well as fully fine-tuned CNNs four orders of magnitude larger.
