Machine learning algorithms to infer trait‐matching and predict species interactions in ecological networks

Maximilian Pichler, Virginie Boreux, Alexandra‐Maria Klein, Matthias Schleuning, Florian Hartig
Methods in Ecology and Evolution · 2019-11-19 · doi:10.1111/2041-210X.13329
TerrestrialInteraction classificationMethods / toolPeer reviewedPlantsInvertebratesBirdsrandom forestboosted regression treesdeep neural networks#trait-matching#plant-pollinator networks#link prediction

Do species interact because their traits match (a bee's tongue and a flower's shape)? Studies disagreed, and the authors argue part of the reason was overly simple GLMs.

🧂 Caveats: Pollination networks with good trait data; how well this carries over to reef networks with patchy traits and detection bias is open.

Catch-up area: Multimodal AI and interaction networks

Since then: Strydom et al. (2021) laid out a roadmap for predicting interaction networks across space and time, with a small neural-net proof of concept and a methods primer. doi:10.1098/rstb.2021.0063 (https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2021.0063)
