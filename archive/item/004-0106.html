Cat Royale Comprehensive Dataset: A Dataset for Pose Detection, Segmentation, Animal Action Recognition and Scene Understanding

Zane K. J. Hartley, Joseph Ho, Alan Chamberlain, Michael P. Pound, Steve Benford
arXiv · 2026-11-11
TerrestrialVideo / imageryMultimodalBehaviour classificationDatasetPreprintMammalsinstance segmentationpose estimationtemporal action localisationvideo-text retrieval#multi-task benchmark#animal welfare

Hartley, Ho, Chamberlain, Pound & Benford built it from 500+ hours of video from the Cat Royale art-and-research project: 20,000 images with instance masks, 5,000 with multi-subject pose, 800+ action clips and 800+ clips with text descriptions, with benchmarks for each task. Cats, not corals, but linked annotation types like this are rare and exactly what multimodal behaviour models need. arXiv:2611.15601
