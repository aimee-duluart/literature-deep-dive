The repeated evolution of anti-bat bioacoustics in hawkmoths

Paul K. Masonick, Maja Petricic, Alessandro Giusti, Christian D. Couch, Jesse R. Barber, Akito Y. Kawahara
Proceedings of the Royal Society B: Biological Sciences · 2026-11-04 · doi:10.1098/rspb.2026.2303
TerrestrialAcousticsInteraction classificationEmpirical / comparativePeer reviewedInvertebratesMammalsacoustic recordingsmicro-CTSEMphylogenetics#predator-prey arms race#anti-bat defence#stridulation

Masonick, Petricic, Giusti, Couch, Barber & Kawahara combined acoustic recordings, electron microscopy, micro-CT and a time-calibrated phylogeny. They describe three stridulatory systems (scale-on-scale, scale-on-spine, scale-on-nodule).
