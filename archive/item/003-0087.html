Iannarilli … Cagnacci and 160+ researchers: 90,230 camera-trap days across 22–23 countries, with 94,441 observations of 63 mammal species and 5,024 detections of 78 bird species. Every record was human-checked after AI classification, and the data are CC BY-SA 4.0. doi:10.1002/ecy.70516
