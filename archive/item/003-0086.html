WhaleSonic: Recordings of cetacean vocal behaviour with hydrophone arrays in Northern Norway

Jörg Rychen, Arvid Berg, Jerome Cohen, Isabel Driscoll, Alexander Eckerle, Valeria Falletta, Olga Filatova, Lena Gies, Pascale Hatt, Adrian Hiss, Franziska Krimmer, Corinna Lorenz, Joseph G. Mine, Charlotte Mommsen, Florian S. Schmidt, Rik L.E.M. Ubaghs, Kaja Wierucka, Richard Hahnloser
openRxiv · 2026-11-03 · doi:10.64898/2026.11.02.763576
MarineAcousticsMonitoringInteraction classificationDatasetPreprintMarine mammalshydrophone arrayslocalisation#killer whales#humpback whales#group communication#Norway

Rychen … Hahnloser: four drifting tetrahedral-array buoys (78 h) plus seven bottom hydrophones (52 days), recorded while killer, humpback, fin and sperm whales shared a fjord in winter 2023–24. Recordings are time-synced and positioned to 2 m.
