AquaProbe: Efficient Adaptation of Frozen Vision Models for Underwater Images

Md Shihabul Islam Shovo, Nishi Kanta Paul, Trung Dong, Xiaomin Lin
arXiv · 2026-10-29
MarineVideo / imagerySpecies detectionMethods / toolPreprintLoRAparameter-efficient fine-tuningdomain adaptation#underwater segmentation#domain shift#SUIM

Shovo, Paul, Dong & Lin measure where in a frozen encoder the underwater gap appears (it shifts with water type and range), then allocate LoRA ranks to match. With the same 0.59M adapter parameters, proportional allocation reached 70.1 mIoU, 1.9 points above uniform.
