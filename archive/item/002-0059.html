Pemba's community co-managed MPAs, 1,300 km² of reef, mangrove and seagrass (Mongabay, 29 Oct)

Coral reefMarinePlants#MPAs#co-management#30x30#Tanzania

Two new conservation areas off Pemba (Zanzibar, Tanzania) were gazetted in June 2026 after work with more than 20 communities. Blue Alliance reports patrols up more than 50% and illegal activity down about 45%.
