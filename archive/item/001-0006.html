A Perch out of Water: Few-Shot Detection of Bryde's Whale Vocalisations in False Bay

G. Tonitz, C. M. Geldenhuys, T. R. Niesler
arXiv · 2026-10-21
MarineAcousticsSpecies detectionMethods / toolMarine mammalsPerchfew-shot learningprototypical networks#baleen whales#embeddings

With ≤10 examples per call type and no fine-tuning, retuning Perch's input (longer frames, mel range restricted to the calls) and per-call detection gained 9.1 AUC points (macro AUC 0.739, Antarctic blue/fin whales). On Bryde's whales in False Bay, Perch beat the whale-specific WhaleVAD for every call type.
