Who Called? V33DA: A Physically Verified Multimodal Benchmark for Vocal Attribution in Zebra Finch Groups

Maris Basha, Yuhang Wang, Xiaoran Chen, Longbiao Cheng, Luca Yapura, Anja T. Zai
arXiv · 2026-10-28
TerrestrialAcousticsVideo / imageryMultimodalInteraction classificationBehaviour classificationDatasetBirdssound source localisation3D poseaccelerometersbenchmarking#vocal attribution#social communication#generalisation#ground truth

Attributing a call to an individual in a group is the core of social-communication research, but it is rarely tested against physical ground truth. V33DA has 33,625 verified events from social zebra finches (10 birds, three experiments).

🧂 Caveats: Preprint. Captive zebra finches in an instrumented arena, not a reef. The abstract reports the gap qualitatively, so check the paper for accuracies.
