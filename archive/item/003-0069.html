Towards ecologically meaningful foundation models

Ross J. Gardiner, Henry Cerbone, Hammed A. Akande, Carly H. Batist, Amber Cowans, Stella M. Felsinger, Premdeep S. Gill, Taniya Kapoor, Eliot T. Miller, Rodrigo Oyanedel, Steven Reece, Yan Ying Tan, Jonas Antony, Carlos Rodriguez-Pardo, Amy Hinsley, Micah Bowles, Sebastian A. Heilpern, Rachel H. Parkinson
Nature Machine Intelligence · 2026-11-06 · doi:10.1038/s42256-026-01317-6
TerrestrialMarineFreshwaterMultimodalInteraction classificationMonitoringPerspectivePeer reviewedfoundation modelsmultimodal learningtheory-guided ML#ecoFMs#generalisation#ecological forecasting

The authors argue that ecological foundation models (ecoFMs) should be trained on large multimodal ecological datasets and informed by ecological theory. The goal is shared representations of organisms, environments and interactions, which could improve generalisation and link pattern to process.

🧂 Caveats: A perspective, so no new results. “Theory-guided” foundation models are mostly an aspiration so far, and evaluation is the hard part.
