Conflict with industrial fleets undermines the contributions of small-scale fisheries to sustainable development in vulnerable regions

Cronin, Finkbeiner, Virdin et al. built a conflict-interaction index. Vulnerability peaks where small-scale fisheries are vital for nutrition but governance is weak (especially Africa, Oceania and Asia).
