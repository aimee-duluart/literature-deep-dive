Recording session leakage inflates accuracy in marine mammal call classification

Erzsebet Frey
EcoEvoRxiv · 2026-11-13 · doi:10.32942/x2p68x
MarinePolarAcousticsSpecies detectionEvaluationPreprintMarine mammalsgrouped cross-validationrandom foresttransformerBirdNETPerch#data leakage#evaluation#Watkins database

Frey classified 4,761 recordings of nine Arctic and subarctic marine mammal species from the Watkins Marine Mammal Sound Database (recorded on 148 tapes, 1949–1997). Models: a random forest on 90 acoustic features, a small transformer and linear probes on pretrained BirdNET and Perch 2.0 embeddings.

🧂 Caveats: A single-author preprint on one archival database, where tape and species are tightly confounded (some species sit on only a few tapes), so the size of the gap is probably at the high end. The direction of the effect, though, is well established.
