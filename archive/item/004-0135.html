Global birdsong embeddings enable superior transfer learning for bioacoustic classification

Burooj Ghani, Tom Denton, Stefan Kahl, Holger Klinck
Scientific Reports · 2023-12-18 · doi:10.1038/s41598-023-49989-z
TerrestrialMarineAcousticsSpecies detectionMethods / toolPeer reviewedBirdsMarine mammalsAmphibiansMammalsembeddingstransfer learningfew-shot learning#bioacoustics#Perch#BirdNET

The constructive ending. Instead of training from scratch on scarce labels, the authors used feature embeddings from large audio classifiers to learn new classes from a few examples: bird call types and dialects, bats, marine mammals and amphibians. Embeddings from models trained on bird vocalisations consistently beat those trained on general audio, even for non-bird taxa.

🧂 Caveats: Transfer quality still varies by taxon and dataset, and few-shot results are only as honest as the split they're scored on (see above).
