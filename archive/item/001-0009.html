Marine heat waves in vulnerable coral reef regions are skillfully predictable on multi-year timescales

Alexia Karwat, June-Yi Lee, Christian L. E. Franzke, Yong-Yub Kim, Eun Young Kwon, Sun-Seon Lee
Nature Communications · 2026-10-27 · doi:10.1038/s41467-026-78279-1
Coral reefMarineRemote sensingMonitoringEmpirical / fieldCoralsCESM2 hindcastsclimate prediction#marine heatwaves#forecasting#ENSO

Karwat, Lee, Franzke et al. used CESM2 five-year hindcasts (1981–2020) across 12 reef regions. Heatwave frequency and cumulative intensity are predictable months to multiple years ahead in many basins, mostly thanks to anthropogenic warming.
