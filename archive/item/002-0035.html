A Scalable Workflow for Extracting Species Interactions From Ecological Literature Using Large Language Models

François Keck, Henry Broadbent, Florian Altermatt
Ecology and Evolution · 2026-10-27 · doi:10.1002/ece3.74626
TerrestrialMarineFreshwaterTextInteraction classificationMethods / toolPeer reviewedlarge language modelsGPT-4oinformation extraction#interaction networks#literature mining#data synthesis

A replicable workflow uses GPT-4o to extract a broad range of biological interactions (not just feeding) directly from the literature. Run on 83,910 articles, it produced a network of 144,402 interactions among 36,471 taxa.

🧂 Caveats: Recall of 70% means roughly 3 in 10 reported interactions are missed. Literature bias (well-studied taxa and regions) carries straight into the network. It relies on a proprietary model.
