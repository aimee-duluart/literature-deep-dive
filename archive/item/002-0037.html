A richness-sensitive Acoustic Biodiversity Index from species-level detections: classifier benchmarking, false-positive control and validation against standard soundscape indices in an African savanna and an urban zoo

Muhammad Saeed, Dmitry Mikhaylov, Mohamed H. Alhosani, Yasser F. Al Wahedi, Rory Pilossof, Ahmed Arabi
arXiv · 2026-10-30
TerrestrialAcousticsSpecies detectionMonitoringMethods / toolPreprintBirdsBirdNETembeddingslogistic regressiongrouped cross-validation#acoustic indices#data leakage#false positives#biodiversity indicators

Classic acoustic indices summarise sound energy, not species. This ABI is Shannon diversity of species-level detections, normalised by the regional species pool expected for the site and season.

🧂 Caveats: Preprint, birds only, two sites. The index inherits classifier errors, so it depends on a good regional species list and false-positive control.
